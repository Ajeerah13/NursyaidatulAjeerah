Hobby

Cafe hunting is one of my favorite hobbies. I enjoy visiting different cafes and exploring their unique concepts, interior designs, and signature menus. So far, I have visited several interesting cafes in different locations, each offering a unique atmosphere and experience.

Besides trying new food and beverages, I enjoy observing cafe aesthetics, taking photos of beautiful interiors, and discovering creative ideas used in cafe designs. Cafe hunting allows me to relax, explore new places, and create memorable experiences with family and friends. It also helps me appreciate different cultures and trends in the food and beverage industry.
